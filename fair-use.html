Fair Use Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Your IntegrateAI subscription includes the AI usage your business needs for normal day-to-day use: the nightly checks, the weekday morning briefs, and answers to your team's questions. This page explains what "AI usage included" means.

Built for your business

IntegrateAI is provided for your business purposes only. Your AI advisers answer questions about your business, and may politely decline requests that are personal, household or otherwise unrelated to it.

Usage limits

We apply automatic daily and monthly usage limits to keep the service reliable and affordable for every client. Limits are set so that normal business use stays within them.

If your use is unusually high

  • Before we apply any limit beyond the automatic ones, we will talk to you first.
  • After that, for the rest of that month, we may limit how often the advisers respond or pause non-essential activity.
  • If your needs have grown, we may offer a plan that suits your usage.

Limits applied under this policy do not change your monthly fee.

Your contract

Your agreement with us sets out the full terms. If anything on this page differs from your agreement, your agreement applies.
