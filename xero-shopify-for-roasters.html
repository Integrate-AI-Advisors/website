Guide for specialty coffee roasters

Xero and Shopify, on one page.

Orders live in Shopify and money lives in Xero. For a roaster, the work is making sure the two tell the same story.

Where the two drift apart

An online order is not the same thing as money in the bank. Between the two sit payment fees, payouts that arrive in batches, refunds, and subscription renewals that were charged on a different day from the one you expected. Wholesale adds another gap: those invoices are raised in Xero, not in your shop at all.

None of this is wrong. It is just several systems each recording their own version of the same week.

A simple weekly routine

  • Compare the week’s online orders with the week’s payouts, and note anything that does not line up.
  • Look at unpaid wholesale invoices in Xero and note which are past their usual payment time.
  • Check that refunds in the shop have been reflected in the books.
  • Check that subscription renewals show up where you expect them.
  • Write down what you could not explain, and look at it again next week.

Twenty minutes a week beats a stressful month end. The trouble is that it only happens if someone remembers to do it.
