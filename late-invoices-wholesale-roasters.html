Guide for specialty coffee roasters

Catch late wholesale invoices early.

A late invoice is rarely a surprise in hindsight. The signal was there days earlier, if anyone was looking.

Why late invoices hurt a roaster

A roaster pays for green coffee, packaging and wages well before a wholesale customer pays for the bags. When invoices run late, the gap is filled from your own cash. A few of them at once and the month feels tight, even when the order book looks healthy.

A fixed rule misses the early warning

Many businesses chase at 30 days. But customers have habits. One café always pays within ten days. Another always takes five weeks, and always pays. A flat rule chases the second one too early and the first one far too late.

What matters is the change: this customer usually pays within ten days, and this invoice is now 28 days late. That is the one to pick up the phone about today.

A routine that works

  • Once a week, list every unpaid wholesale invoice.
  • For each, ask: is this later than this customer normally is?
  • Start with a friendly note. Most late payments are an oversight.
  • If there is no reply, pick up the phone.
  • Write down who tends to be late, so next time you know.
