Guide for specialty coffee roasters

One dashboard for the whole roastery.

Most roasteries run on a dozen tools. Each one has its own dashboard, and the only place they all meet is the owner’s head.

Why the numbers live in different places

Online orders sit in Shopify. Subscriptions sit in Skio. Payments land in Stripe, invoices are raised in Xero, roasts are logged in Cropster, and parcels travel with Royal Mail or DPD. Each tool answers its own question well. None of them answers the question an owner actually asks first thing: how is the business doing this morning?

The usual fix is a spreadsheet, or a mental tour of six logins. Both work until the week you are busy, which is exactly the week something slips.

What one screen should show

  • Money in over the last 30 days, split into wholesale, online shop and subscriptions.
  • Who owes you money, and how late they are compared with how they normally pay.
  • Which wholesale accounts have gone quiet.
  • Which subscriptions look at risk.
  • Whether every connected tool is up to date, so you can trust what you are looking at.

What IntegrateAI does

IntegrateAI brings sales, finance, roasting, delivery and customer data from the tools a roaster already uses into one mission control. Every night an AI team checks the data. Each weekday morning you get a short brief in Slack and on your mission control, showing what changed and the few things that need you.

If you want to know something else, ask in Slack or on your mission control and you will usually get an answer in about a minute.
