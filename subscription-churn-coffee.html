Guide for specialty coffee roasters

Spot a subscriber before they leave.

Subscribers rarely cancel without warning. They go quiet first, and quiet is easy to miss.

Why subscriptions are worth protecting

A subscriber who stays is worth far more than the first bag they bought. Subscriptions are steady, predictable income for a roaster, which is why losing one hurts more than the single order suggests.

What a subscription at risk can look like

The signals differ from one roaster to the next, and no single sign is proof. Some common ones to watch for:

  • A run of skipped or paused deliveries.
  • A payment that failed and was not retried successfully.
  • A delivery that went wrong, or a parcel that arrived late.
  • A customer who used to open your emails and no longer does.

Each of these sits in a different tool: the subscription platform, the payment provider, the courier and the email platform. That is why they are so easy to miss one at a time.

A routine that works

  • Once a week, look for subscribers whose pattern has changed.
  • Reach out personally to the ones who matter most, with a friendly check-in rather than a discount.
  • Fix the cause if there is one: a failed payment, a late parcel.
  • Note why people leave, and look for patterns.

Where IntegrateAI helps
