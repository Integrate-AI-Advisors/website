1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how we handle personal data collected through our website at integrate-ai.uk — principally the email address you can give us to request the sample diagnostic. It is written to comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

It does not cover how we handle personal data inside a paid client engagement — that is governed by the contract and data-processing terms we agree with each client directly.

2. What data we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us, plus a small amount of technical information needed to run the site safely.

Information you give us

If you ask us to send you the sample diagnostic, we collect the one field the form asks for: your email address. That's it — no company details, no revenue figures, no tool checklist.

The form also contains a hidden "honeypot" field that is invisible to people and only filled in by automated spam tools. If it is completed, we discard the submission. No genuine visitor data is collected by this field.

If you email us directly, or book a call, we hold whatever you choose to put in that email or booking. We do not ask for special-category data (such as health, ethnicity, or political views), and we ask you not to include any.

Information we collect automatically

Our hosting provider (Vercel) processes basic server logs, including IP addresses, as a necessary part of delivering and protecting the website. These logs are short-lived and used only for security and reliability. We collect nothing else.

3. Cookies & analytics

We set no cookies and we run no analytics.

No Google Analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising tags, no third-party scripts of any kind. We don't measure you, and we don't build a profile of you. That's why you'll never see a cookie banner on this site — there's nothing to consent to.

If that ever changes, we'll ask for your consent first, and this page will say so before anything loads.

4. How we use your data

We use the information you give us to:

  • Send you the sample diagnostic you asked for
  • Arrange, confirm, and prepare for a diagnostic call you have requested
  • Respond to enquiries you send us by email

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you. We will only send you marketing if you have asked us to or are an existing client, and you can opt out at any time.

6. Who we share your data with

We keep your data within IntegrateAI and a small number of carefully chosen service providers ("sub-processors") who help us run the business. Each only receives the data needed for their specific job, and each is bound to protect it.

ProviderWhat they handle
Google (Gmail)Carries the sample-diagnostic email we send you, and the notification to us. Your email address passes through Gmail.
Cal.comOur booking platform. When you schedule a diagnostic call, the name, email and time you provide are processed by Cal.com. See section 7.
Google (Calendar)A booked call creates a calendar event and confirmation emails through Google Calendar and Gmail.

We may also disclose data where we are legally required to (for example, to comply with a court order), or to professional advisers such as our accountant. We will never sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

7. Booking your call (Cal.com & Google)

If you book a diagnostic call, you'll do it on our booking page, powered by Cal.com. The details you enter there — typically your name, email address, and chosen time — are submitted to Cal.com, which acts as a third-party processor on our behalf.

Once a booking is made, a calendar event and confirmation emails are created using Google services (Google Calendar and Gmail). This means your name, email, and the meeting details are also processed by Google so we can hold the call and remind you about it.

Both Cal.com and Google operate their own privacy policies. You can read Cal.com's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy for how they handle data as part of providing these services.

8. How long we keep your data

  • Your email address, if you requested the sample: kept while we're in contact and for up to 24 months after our last exchange, then deleted. We don't add you to a mailing list.
  • Enquiry and booking details: kept while we are in contact and for up to 24 months after our last interaction, so we can follow up sensibly — then deleted, unless you become a client.
  • Client records: retained for the duration of the engagement and for up to 6 years afterwards to meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.
  • Server logs: short-lived, typically retained for a matter of days.

9. International transfers

Some of our providers (including Google, Cal.com, and Vercel) are based in or process data in the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside the UK, it is protected by appropriate safeguards — such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or Standard Contractual Clauses — so your data receives an equivalent level of protection.

10. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how we use your data (this policy)
  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify data that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten") where there is no overriding reason to keep it
  • Restrict how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to any direct marketing
  • Data portability — receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting prior lawful processing

To exercise any of these, email hello@integrate-ai.uk. We will respond within one month and we will not charge you for a reasonable request.

11. How we protect your data

We use reputable providers with strong security practices, limit access to your data to the two of us, and only keep what we need for as long as we need it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access.

12. Children

Our website and services are aimed at businesses and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, if we add a new tool or service. When we make material changes we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Please check back occasionally.

14. Contact & complaints

For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, email us at hello@integrate-ai.uk.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the UK supervisory authority, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first, so please do contact us before you do.
