Where the money is

Four places a food & drink business leaks or grows

The workforce watches all four at once and brings you the moves. These are illustrative — built around a roaster, but the same four hold for a bakery, a brewery or any independent producer.

Save money

Your roaster sat idle for 90 minutes between batches. Grouping the runs saves gas and an hour of an operator’s time — about £38 a run, with no customer impact. The workforce spots the gap and drafts a tighter schedule for your head roaster to confirm.

Grow revenue

A new wholesale lead enquires about your single origins. The workforce drafts the intro, offers a sample box, and adds your wholesale terms once you ask — so the upsell goes out warm and same-day instead of sitting in a drafts folder for a week.

Get efficient

Sales, stock, accounts and wholesale orders all land in one Slack thread. No flicking between five tabs to know where you stand. One morning brief at 06:30, then approvals as the day needs them — the business reports to you, not the other way round.

Keep customers

A wholesale account that orders every fortnight has gone quiet for five weeks. That pattern usually means churn. The workforce flags it early and drafts a friendly check-in for your wholesale lead — before the account quietly switches roaster.
