## 5 · It acts

**Section label:** 03 — ACTS

**Headline:** And then it gets on with the work.

**Body:** Not suggestions in a report you'll never read. Work, done: the overdue invoice
chased, the wholesale enquiry answered, the reorder queued, the quiet regular won back.
Four things it never stops working on —

**More customers.** The regulars who went quiet mostly didn't fall out of love — they hit
a failed payment. That's billing, not taste. The Brain spots it and wins them back.

**More time.** The ninety-minute Monday pile — reconciling orders, chasing signatures,
updating the sheet — is done before you've finished your coffee.

**More revenue.** The lead you forgot to follow up, the reorder that's due, the quote you
never sent — it sees them, and it acts.

**Less waste.** You're about to over-order stock again because last month *felt* busy.
The numbers say otherwise. It flags it before the cash goes out the door.

## 9 · Why not just…?

**…use ChatGPT?** Brilliant in a chat window — but it isn't wired into your tools, and it
can't do a thing once you close the tab. The Brain is connected to your business and works
while you sleep.

**…set up Zapier?** A zap is one fixed step you wired up in advance. The Brain works out
what needs doing and does it — no flowcharts, no maintenance.

**…hire a part-time VA?** One person, a few hours a week, who still needs briefing and
managing. The Brain is always on, already knows everything, and costs a fraction.

### Questions founders actually ask

- **How long does setup take?** Days, not months. We connect your main tools and it
  starts learning straight away.
- **What if it gets something wrong?** It asks before anything that matters, and every
  action is logged and reversible. You're never handing over the keys.
- **Am I locked in?** No. Switch it off whenever you like — your data comes with you, and
  it forgets. `[DPA]`
- **Is my data safe?** UK company, signed data agreement, never used to train models —
  see above. `[DPA]`
